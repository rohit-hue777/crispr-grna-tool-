On-target is a rule-of-thumb score (GC balance, 3′ base, seed GC, homopolymers), not a trained model such as Rule Set 2 or DeepSpCas9. Specificity uses the MIT (Hsu 2013) mismatch weights, exact for SpCas9 with 20-nt guides and approximate otherwise, and only sees sequences you paste. It is not a genome-wide search, so confirm your final picks in CRISPOR or Cas-OFFinder before ordering.
